The Magliola Workshops were a joint-stock company that operated in

the construction and repair of rail vehicles, carrying out

revision, restructuring, and modification work on railway and tram

vehicles of all kinds (both passenger and freight), updating safety

systems to modern standards.

In 1901, Magliola opened new workshops next to the Santhià station,

located on the Turin-Milan line. In 2014, the consortium was

declared bankrupt.
