Today I spent the day in bed.

I didn't even have the strength to watch the world go by outside the window.

I cried over improbable songs, probably because of a slight fever.

It's a bad feeling, not being able to find yourself anymore.

It's true, a thousand things have happened in these years; I certainly don't want to go back, especially to the period when I was emptied of my greatest love: photography.

Tonight I'll watch a Pixar movie.
