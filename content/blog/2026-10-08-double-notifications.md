I treat notifications on my phone as a task list. I have my phone at my desk
and I see the screen light up, and at the back of my mind I know there's
something up I have to look up later. Since I have notifications trimmed down
to the essential, this is a good heuristic for me.

For most of my communication needs with friends/family, I use iMessage, and the
biggest annoyance, starting from (I think) iOS 16 for me (or the last time I
set-up an iPhone from scratch), but I found references of this functionality in
the [iPhone OS 3.1 User
Guide](https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/6GJYWVAV/user/ma616_iphone_ios3_1_user_guide.pdf)(!!!),
was a second notification for every message that got sent to me, delivered
after exactly 2 minutes.

[Francesco](https://nomnp.com) and I were about to go crazy for this. 

Today I learned this is a "feature" (and I'm generous with my language here) of
iMessages.

To keep my mental sanity:

```
Settings -> Notifications -> Messages -> Customize Notifications -> Repeat Alerts -> Never
```
