Lately, I'm suffering whenever I'm ready to press the shutter on my camera. It's a strange feeling because I might even see a scene that I'd like to shoot, but then my gut says not to press the shutter, and what I get is zero pictures.

Last Saturday, I loaded another roll of film into my M6 after finishing one just for the sake of it, it lasted all summer, 36 shots of Gold 200, and I haven't taken any shots on it yet (even though I had the good part of Saturday afternoon, the whole Sunday, and Monday evening). Heck, I haven't even decided what ISO I want to shoot it at!

I'm in a creative rut, and if I look back, I think I haven't shot anything I'm happy with since May.
