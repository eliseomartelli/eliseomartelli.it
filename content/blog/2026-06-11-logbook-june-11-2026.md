Sometimes you go out into the streets with a camera, walking, suffering through the heat and cold, dealing with pain, and maybe you shoot nothing.

Sometimes I wonder why I decided to dedicate a big part of my life to this craft, but then there are days when I get to play soccer with kids on the street, or I get to talk to strangers.

I don't even consider myself a good photographer, but the camera is such a great passport to enjoying life.
