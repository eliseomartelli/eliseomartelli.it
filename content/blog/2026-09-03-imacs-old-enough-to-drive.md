Yesterday, Francesco and I brought home two 2008 iMacs, the ones with the
Core 2 Duo processors. One comes straight from a local radio station, while
the other is from the desk of a graphic design studio. These machines
inevitably show their age but I think they are still charming.

The real dilemma now is figuring out how to give mine a meaningful second
life. I have four main options on the table:

1. Home Assistant Dashboard. Write a dedicated application to act as an
   MQTT bridge and turn the screen into a smart home display. It might be a
   bit of a waste of potential for a machine like this, but it would be
   functional for my smart home.
2. OpenBSD playground. Wipe the drive and turn it into a dedicated OpenBSD
   machine.
3. Offline Writing Machine. Strictly disconnected from the internet. The
   goal is to create a distraction-free, notification-free environment that
   is perfect for writing posts for this blog.
4. Retro Media Hub. Restore it to use exclusively as a physical CD player
   and for syncing iPods.
