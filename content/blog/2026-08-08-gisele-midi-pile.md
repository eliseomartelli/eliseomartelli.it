Last Thursday, my brother and I were driving and decided to put on the FM
radio.

We don't usually listen to it because of the audio quality, but it suited the
chill drive. After a few songs, we heard something that sounded off. Synthetic.
I looked into it later, and it turns out my intuition was right: we were
listening to a song built by artificial intelligence.

The song is "Dimanche Midi Pile" by Gisèle. It was released in early July 2026,
published and distributed directly by Warner Music Italy.

As of this week, this artificial track is sitting in the top 20 most aired
tracks on Italian radio. I find it utterly disgusting and disrespectful to
human artists and listeners.

Gisèle does not exist. She is a brand persona created entirely by artificial
intelligence.

The song's composer, writer, and prompter is credited as "Valerio Pastore".
Pastore, allegedly, is a sound engineer and technician who is well-connected in
the Italian pop scene (and widely known as the partner of Italian pop superstar
Alessandra Amoroso).

What makes this release particularly insidious is the lack of transparency:

- On digital platforms like YouTube, Warner Music Italy includes a small,
  legally-required disclaimer indicating the video contains "altered or
  AI-generated content."
- On the radio, however, this transparency disappears. The track has charted on
  EarOne (the official Italian radio airplay monitoring service) in the
  international and TV airplay tables. Radio hosts introduce the track simply
  as "the new single by Gisèle," presenting it alongside real, human artists as
  if she were a flesh-and-blood singer.

I hate the world we are building.
