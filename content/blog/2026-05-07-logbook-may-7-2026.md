Yesterday morning I watched a puddle drying up, taken by force by the sun's
rays.  
A "necessary" violence to make room for the next puddle.

Transformation is an act of rupture, and at this moment, I feel I am in a phase
of transformation.

Today I feel a bit like that puddle: a bit mortified, a bit disappointed, and a
bit of a disappointment.

Does evaporating mean failing?
