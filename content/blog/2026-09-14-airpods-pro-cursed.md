Do you remember the AirPods Pro [I
saved](/blog/2026-06-24-airpods-pro-case-revived/) back in June by replacing
the battery in the case? Yesterday, the earbuds took a spin in the washing
machine.

Currently, they are trapped in a pairing glitch. The white LED on the case
flashes continuously without stopping, rejecting any attempt to pair. A
somewhat ironic ending.

Now all that is left is for me to figure out if a little patience, some
silica gel and some sun can work a miracle to dry them out internally, or if
this time the water has permanently short-circuited the case's motherboard.
